# Development roadmap

1. Foundation: authentication, database, hostel structure, students.
2. Core operations: allocation, attendance, leave, complaints.
3. AI: complaint analysis, room recommendation, roommate scoring, attendance and fee analytics, occupancy forecasting, chatbot.
4. Administration: fees/payments, visitors, mess, notices, notifications, audit logs.
5. Reporting: PDF/CSV/Excel reports and analytics dashboards.
6. Quality: unit/integration tests, Postman collection, Docker, documentation, diagrams.
7. Demonstration: follow the supplied admin-to-reports demo flow.
